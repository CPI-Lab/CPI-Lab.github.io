## Overview

**CIPHER (Cybersecurity Intelligence, Protection and Holistic Enterprise Resilience)** is a European research and innovation project dedicated to strengthening the cybersecurity and resilience of critical infrastructures.

Funded by the European Union's Digital Europe Programme, CIPHER aims to develop a collaborative, standardised, and service-oriented experimentation platform that enables organisations to assess cybersecurity strategies, identify vulnerabilities, and validate protection mechanisms against evolving cyber threats.

The project brings together research institutions, technology providers, critical infrastructure operators, and cybersecurity specialists to advance proactive security, trustworthy information sharing, and regulatory compliance.

## Research Objectives

CIPHER focuses on six technical objectives:

1. **Collaborative Experimentation Platform:** Develop a cybersecurity experimentation environment supporting cloud-to-edge infrastructures and realistic cyberattack simulations.

2. **Dynamic Risk and Threat Assessment:** Integrate threat intelligence, contextual information, and advanced analytics to identify and assess emerging cybersecurity risks.

3. **Automated Penetration Testing:** Develop automated security testing mechanisms to identify vulnerabilities and support continuous security assessment.

4. **Security Countermeasures and Response:** Investigate and orchestrate security controls, monitoring services, and mitigation strategies to improve organisational preparedness.

5. **Trustworthy Information Sharing:** Enable secure collaboration across organisational boundaries through data provenance, distributed access control, and trustworthy information exchange.

6. **Security Advisory and Compliance:** Support cybersecurity assessments, auditing, and compliance with European regulatory frameworks, including NIS2 and DORA.

## Research and Innovation

CIPHER investigates integrated cybersecurity mechanisms for distributed and collaborative digital environments.

Its technical approach combines cyber-range experimentation, automated penetration testing, continuous risk monitoring, and intelligent security orchestration to support proactive threat detection and response.

A key research direction concerns **trustworthy cross-organisational information sharing**, where decentralised identity management, distributed access control, and data provenance mechanisms enable organisations to collaborate without compromising security, accountability, or control over shared resources.

The project also explores the integration of cybersecurity intelligence and automated assessment mechanisms to improve the adaptability and resilience of critical digital infrastructures.

## Use Cases

CIPHER validates its technologies through three industrial pilot domains:

- **Telecommunications:** Improving cybersecurity preparedness, threat monitoring, and incident response in complex telecommunications infrastructures.

- **Logistics:** Assessing cybersecurity risks and protection mechanisms in interconnected logistics operations and supply chains.

- **Water Utilities:** Strengthening the security and resilience of critical water infrastructure, including interconnected IT and operational technology environments.

These pilots provide representative environments for evaluating the project's security capabilities, interoperability, and practical applicability.

## CPI Lab Contributions

Within CIPHER, **TU Delft's Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab)** contributes to research on trustworthy identity management, distributed access control, and secure information sharing across organisational boundaries.

The research investigates mechanisms for context-aware authorisation, continuous access evaluation, and auditable security decisions in collaborative digital environments.

These activities contribute to the development of trustworthy cybersecurity mechanisms that support secure collaboration, accountability, and resilience across distributed infrastructures.
