## Join CPI Lab

We are always interested in connecting with talented researchers and students who share our interest in cyber-physical intelligence, trustworthy systems, distributed intelligence, and next-generation digital infrastructures.

## Open Positions

There are currently **no open positions** at CPI Lab.

New PhD, postdoctoral, research, and student opportunities will be published here when they become available.

## Interested in working with us?

Even when no positions are currently advertised, prospective researchers and students are welcome to follow the activities of CPI Lab and explore opportunities at Delft University of Technology.

Vacancies associated with CPI Lab may include:

- PhD positions
- Postdoctoral researcher positions
- Research and engineering positions
- MSc thesis projects
- BSc thesis projects
- Research internships
- Visiting researcher opportunities

For officially advertised positions at TU Delft, please consult the [TU Delft vacancies website](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/working-at-tu-delft/search-jobs).
