CPI Lab welcomes contact from researchers, students, public organisations, and industry partners interested in collaboration around cyber-physical intelligence, trustworthy systems, distributed computing, AI, networking, security, and data-driven infrastructures.

## Collaborate with us

We are interested in building collaborations around research projects, scientific publications, joint proposals, industrial challenges, and student supervision.

Typical forms of collaboration include:

* Joint research projects and grant proposals
* Academic and industrial research partnerships
* PhD, MSc, and BSc supervision
* Visiting researchers and research exchanges
* Technology validation and experimental pilots
* Workshops, seminars, and invited talks
* Collaboration around datasets, platforms, and research infrastructure

If you have a research question, project idea, or challenge that aligns with the work of CPI Lab, we would be happy to hear from you.

## Research and industry partnerships

CPI Lab works across academic, public-sector, and industrial environments. We are particularly interested in collaborations that involve complex cyber-physical systems, distributed intelligence, trustworthy AI, secure data sharing, digital infrastructures, and real-world deployment scenarios.

If your organisation is exploring a research collaboration or would like to discuss participation in a future project, please contact us with a short description of the topic, expected collaboration, and relevant context.

## Students and researchers

Prospective MSc and BSc students may contact us regarding thesis opportunities, research projects, and supervision.

Researchers interested in visiting CPI Lab, collaborating on publications, or developing joint research activities are also welcome to get in touch.

Open positions are published on our [Vacancies](/vacancies/) page when available.

## Contact

For general enquiries and collaboration proposals, please contact one of the CPI Lab directors or principal investigators through their profile pages.

You can find the full team on the [Members](/members/) page.

CPI Lab is based at Delft University of Technology in Delft, the Netherlands.
