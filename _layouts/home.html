About CPI Lab

Intelligence at the intersection of the cyber and physical worlds.

The Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab) at Delft University of Technology conducts research on the design, development, and operation of intelligent and trustworthy cyber-physical systems.

Our work brings together distributed intelligence, computing, networking, data, and security to enable next-generation digital infrastructures and autonomous systems.

Research

Research areas

We investigate fundamental and applied challenges across interconnected intelligent systems.

01

Cyber-Physical Systems

Architectures connecting computational intelligence with physical environments and processes.

02

Edge & Distributed AI

Distributed intelligence across edge devices, infrastructure, and collaborative environments.

03

Trustworthy AI

Secure, accountable, explainable, and resilient intelligent systems.

04

Internet of Things

Connected devices and systems supporting intelligent, data-driven physical environments.

05

Networking & Security

Resilient communication, cybersecurity, identity, access control, and trustworthy infrastructures.

06

Digital Twins & Data Spaces

Trusted data exchange and digital representations supporting collaborative cyber-physical ecosystems.
