Martijn Warnier is Full Professor of Complex System Design at Delft University of Technology and an Affiliated Fellow of the Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab). He is a member of the Systems Engineering Section within the Department of Multi-Actor Systems at the Faculty of Technology, Policy and Management.

His research investigates how complex behaviours emerge in socio-technical systems and how such systems can be designed and managed to improve their resilience, reliability, security, and operational efficiency.

His work combines computational modelling, simulation, and self-organisation techniques to develop adaptive mechanisms for complex systems. A particular emphasis is placed on intelligent distributed systems, multi-agent systems, and the design of infrastructures that can adapt to changing operational and environmental conditions.

His research spans several application domains, including energy infrastructures, smart grids, cloud computing, sensor networks, transportation networks, and supply chains. He also investigates the security, privacy, and societal implications of distributed and interconnected technologies.

He obtained his PhD in Computer Science from Radboud University Nijmegen in 2006. He is also affiliated with the TU Delft Digital Ethics Centre, the PowerWeb initiative, and the Dutch research school SIKS, and is a member of IEEE and ACM.
