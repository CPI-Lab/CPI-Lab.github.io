Cilia Baanstra is a PhD researcher at Delft University of Technology and a member of the Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab).

Her research is situated within the interdisciplinary environment of the Faculty of Technology, Policy and Management, where technological developments are examined alongside organisational and societal challenges.

Through her work at TU Delft, she contributes to academic research and collaboration within the laboratory's broader research community.
