Ana-Maria Oprescu is an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at the University of Amsterdam (UvA) and an Affiliated Fellow of the Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab). She is a member of the Complex CyberInfrastructure research group within the Informatics Institute.

Her research focuses on understanding the principles underlying the complexity of modern software services, with the objective of developing sustainable, controllable, and energy-efficient software systems.

A central theme of her research is energy-driven software engineering, investigating how software design, implementation, and deployment decisions influence energy consumption and environmental sustainability. Her work also explores software quality, testing, and the trade-offs between energy efficiency, privacy, and accuracy in data-driven systems.

Her recent research extends to sustainable artificial intelligence, energy-efficient code generation using Large Language Models (LLMs), federated learning, and energy consumption monitoring in distributed computing infrastructures.

She obtained her PhD in Computer Science from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam in 2013, investigating stochastic approaches to self-adaptive application execution in cloud environments. She also serves as Programme Director of the Software Engineering Master's programme at the University of Amsterdam.
