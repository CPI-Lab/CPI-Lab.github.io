Nitesh Bharosa is Professor at Delft University of Technology. His research focuses on the design, governance, and adoption of digital infrastructures and emerging technologies in the public sector.

His work investigates how governments and public organisations can use digital technologies to improve public services while safeguarding public values. A central theme of his research is GovTech and the development of digital ecosystems in which public and private organisations collaborate to deliver innovative digital services.

His research covers digital identity and trust services, data wallets, digital infrastructures, and their governance. He is particularly interested in the organisational and institutional challenges surrounding the adoption of these technologies and their implications for digital sovereignty, trust, and public value.

His work also addresses the transition towards secure future digital infrastructures, including research on post-quantum cryptography and the organisational and governance challenges involved in migrating critical information infrastructures towards quantum-safe technologies.
