Thomas van Binsbergen is an Associate Professor in Computer Science at the University of Amsterdam (UvA) and an Affiliated Fellow of the Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab). He leads the Programming Languages and Verification team within the Complex-Cyber Infrastructure research group at the Informatics Institute.

His research focuses on the operational semantics of programming languages, with particular emphasis on modular approaches to language specification, design, and implementation. His work explores how formal techniques and domain-specific languages can address challenges across different application domains.

A central aspect of his research is automated legal and normative reasoning. His contributions include the development of eFLINT, a domain-specific language for formally specifying and reasoning about norms, regulations, and obligations within computational systems.

His broader research interests encompass software language engineering, formal verification, programmable networks, and the development of computational mechanisms for interpreting and reasoning about regulatory and legal specifications.
