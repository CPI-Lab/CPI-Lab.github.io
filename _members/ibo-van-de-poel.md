Ibo van de Poel is Antoni van Leeuwenhoek Professor in Ethics and Technology at Delft University of Technology and an Affiliated Fellow of the Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab). He is also a member of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences (KNAW).

His research examines the relationship between technological development, ethical values, and societal change. His work explores how ethical considerations can be systematically incorporated into engineering practices and technological design, with particular emphasis on responsible innovation, moral responsibility, and the acceptability of technological risks.

A central theme of his research is Design for Values, investigating how moral and societal values can inform the design of technologies and how technological developments can, in turn, transform those values. His work also extends to the ethical implications of emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence.

He received an NWO VICI grant in 2010 for his research on technology as a form of societal experimentation and an ERC Advanced Grant in 2018 to investigate value change in socio-technical systems. He is also one of the principal investigators of the Ethics of Socially Disruptive Technologies (ESDiT) research programme.

He holds a Master's degree in Philosophy of Science, Technology and Society and a PhD in Science and Technology Studies from the University of Twente.
