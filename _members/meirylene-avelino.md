Meirylene Avelino is a PhD researcher in Computer Science at Fluminense Federal University (UFF), Brazil, and a visiting researcher at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), within the ICT Section and the Cyber-Physical Intelligence Laboratory (CPI Lab). Her research focuses on cybersecurity, trustworthy data governance, and access and usage control mechanisms for sensitive data, particularly in healthcare environments.

Her work investigates how identity, contextual risk assessment, and policy-based authorisation mechanisms can be combined to ensure that access to sensitive information is secure, traceable, and aligned with organisational policies throughout the data lifecycle.

Her doctoral research explores a holistic framework for sensitive data governance in healthcare environments, integrating risk-based access control, policy enforcement, and continuous auditing. The research also investigates the application of HL7 FHIR and blockchain-based mechanisms to support secure information exchange, accountability, and traceability in healthcare data infrastructures.

Before her doctoral research, she completed a Master's degree in Computer Science at UFF, investigating blockchain-based mechanisms for ensuring the authenticity of web content. She also has professional experience in Information Security, having worked across areas such as Security Operations, Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC), Application Security, and DevSecOps.

Her broader research interests lie at the intersection of cybersecurity, data governance, privacy, and distributed technologies, with particular emphasis on developing security architectures that enable trustworthy collaboration and responsible data sharing across organisational boundaries.
